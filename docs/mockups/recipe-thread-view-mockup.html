Tonkotsu ramen

serves 4 · critical path 6h 15m

1
Spoon 2 tbsp tare ▸ into each warmed bowl.
Tare · makes ½ cup · 10m open ↗
1Simmer ½ cup soy, ¼ cup mirin, 2 tbsp sake, kombu 5 min.
2Off heat, steep katsuobushi 5 min, strain.
2
Ladle in 1.5 cups hot tonkotsu broth ▾ per bowl, whisk to combine with tare.
Tonkotsu broth · makes 1.5 qt · 6h make ahead  open ↗

2Hard boil with aromatics ▸ 6 hours, topping up water.

3Strain. Should be opaque white and coat a spoon.

Chashu pork · makes 8 slices · 3h open ↗
1Roll and tie 1 lb pork belly; sear all sides.
2Braise in soy/mirin/sake 3h at low simmer; chill, slice.
